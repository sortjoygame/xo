Privacy Policy

XO Journey (Android game) · Last updated: September 26, 2026

Summary

XO Journey is an X and O puzzle game. Playing against the computer and 2 players on one phone work offline and need no account. Online play with friends uses our own game server. We do not collect your email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell data.

Data stored on your device

  • journey progress (15 Levels × 25 Games), match statistics and a short match history
  • coins, gems, gifts, your Collection (avatars, designs, boards, backgrounds) and achievements
  • your profile: the name you type (optional), avatar and XP level
  • settings (music, sound effects, volumes, vibration) and small app flags

Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account.

Online play (only when you open Multiplayer)

The app connects over an encrypted connection to our own game server. The server gives you a Player ID (for example XO-48291) and a secret key that stays on your phone; the server keeps only a fingerprint (hash) of that key. It stores your chosen name and avatar, your friends and pending friend requests, your online wins and XP (for leaderboards). Invitations and matches exist only in the server's memory and are removed shortly after they end. Friends and other players can see your name, avatar, Player ID and scores. Please do not use your full real name.

Anonymous statistics and crash reports

To make the game fair and stable we use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They collect anonymous information such as which Game was started, finished or failed and on which try, hints used, whether an optional video was watched, purchase events, device model and Android version, crash reports, approximate country and a random app instance ID. This is never used to personalise ads. It is processed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can switch it off in the game: Settings → "Share anonymous usage stats and crash reports".

Advertising

The first 7 Games show no ads. After that the game can show a small banner on menu screens (never on the game board) and videos that you choose to watch (for a hint or coins). There are no forced pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location (from the IP address), app interactions and diagnostics, according to the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law, the game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice in Settings → Change ad consent. "Remove Ads" removes the banner.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

To remove the data on your phone, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's Settings → Apps → XO Journey. To delete your online profile (Player ID, friends, scores) from our server, email us with your Player ID; we delete it within 30 days. Step by step: Delete your data.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
